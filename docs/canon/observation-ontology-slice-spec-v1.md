# Observation Ontology Slice Spec v1

## Agency States + Metacognitive Moments

## Status

Slice Specification Draft — B-Level Observation Expansion

This document defines the first controlled B-level ontology expansion for the Observation layer.

Scope is intentionally narrow.

This slice introduces:

* Agency States
* Metacognitive Moments

as explicit phenomenological dimensions.

This document is:

* implementation-guiding,
* boundary-defining,
* and runtime-aligned.

It is NOT:

* a full ontology redesign,
* a latent redesign,
* or a reflective-space redesign.

---

# 1. Purpose

This slice exists to improve Lumira’s ability to represent:

* dream agency,
* altered control,
* reflective awareness,
* and dream-state cognition

WITHOUT:

* collapsing into interpretation,
* over-expanding ontology complexity,
* or introducing hidden psychological authority.

The goal is:

# richer phenomenological substrate with stable descriptive boundaries.

# 5. Agency State Philosophy

Agency states describe:

# how action/control is experienced inside the dream.

They are:

* phenomenological,
* not psychological.

Agency observation MUST remain:

* local,
* experiential,
* evidence-linked,
* and ambiguity-preserving.

---

## Recommended medium-granularity types

### Restricted Action

Examples:

* unable to move
* unable to run
* unable to speak
* slowed movement
* blocked escape

---

### Deliberate Action

Examples:

* intentional choice
* conscious attempt
* directed exploration
* protective action
* intentional confrontation

---

### Passive Drift

Examples:

* being carried along
* passive observation
* loss of initiative
* dream-flow drifting

---

### Altered Control

Examples:

* unstable control
* fluctuating agency
* partial control recovery
* involuntary movement
* dream-environment overriding intention

---

### Agency Transition

Examples:

* paralysis becoming movement
* hesitation becoming action
* passive state becoming deliberate engagement

---

# 7. Forbidden Agency Semantics

Observation must NOT infer:

* helpless personality structure
* trauma conclusions
* fear-of-authority explanations
* psychological weakness
* attachment conclusions
* identity narratives

Forbidden examples:

```txt id="0zjlwm"

```

These belong only, if anywhere, to:

# latent probabilistic cognition.

# 8. Metacognitive Moment Philosophy

Metacognitive moments describe:

# awareness-related experiences occurring inside the dream itself.

These are:

* observed reflective events,
* not consciousness theories.
