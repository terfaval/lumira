# Observation Ontology Slice Spec v3

## Spatial Instability + Dream-State Phenomenology

## Status

Slice Specification Draft - B-Level Observation Completion

This document defines the third bounded B-level ontology slice for Observation.

This slice introduces:

- `spatial_instability`
- `dream_state_quality`
- `continuity_fragment`
- `altered_realism`

as first-class descriptive categories.

## Purpose

Improve descriptive capture of dream-space instability and altered realism while preserving non-authoritative semantics.

Observation remains phenomenological. It does not infer metaphysical truth.

## Allowed vs Forbidden Boundary

Allowed:

- "the environment felt unreal"
- "the dream seemed spiritually significant"
- "the scene felt cosmically large"

Forbidden:

- "the dream accessed higher reality"
- "the dream revealed ultimate truth"
- "this was another dimension"

## Category Scope

`spatial_instability`:
- impossible geometry
- looping spaces
- shifting architecture
- broken spatial continuity

`dream_state_quality`:
- false awakening
- explicit still-dreaming recognition
- lucid/partial-lucid state cues
- dream-state instability language

`continuity_fragment`:
- abrupt environmental transition
- scene discontinuity without bridge
- memory-gap style continuity breaks
- wake-then-still-dreaming discontinuity

`altered_realism`:
- felt unrealness
- hyperreal or distorted reality quality
- unstable realism perception

## Extraction Philosophy

Extraction is bounded and uncertainty-first:

- preserve ambiguity
- prefer omission over unsupported structure
- avoid over-triggering metaphoric language
- avoid flattening all unusual language into B3 categories

## Latent Boundary

These categories can be consumed downstream as low-confidence, `internal_only` signals.

They must not backflow into durable Observation truth.

## Migration / Runtime Rules

Implementation remains:

- additive
- isolated
- rollback-safe

No coupling to broad latent redesign or symbolic interpretation.

## Validation Expectations

Required test coverage:

- allowed dream phenomenology cases
- forbidden metaphysical authority cases
- ambiguity-preserving phrasing
- weak-evidence degradation behavior
- extraction edge behavior (boundedness over breadth)
- latent seam directionality preservation

## Final Principle

This slice succeeds when Lumira notices spatial and dream-state strangeness more clearly without becoming interpretive or metaphysically authoritative.
