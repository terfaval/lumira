# Lumira — Observation / Latent / Glossary / Work Redesign Handoff v1

## Purpose

This document summarizes the emerging redesign direction for:

* Observation
* Latent interpretive cognition
* Highlights
* Glossary
* Reflective work/questions

This direction should guide:

* future runtime redesign,
* UX redesign,
* schema redesign,
* and AI behavior.

This is currently:

# conceptual architecture direction,

not implementation spec.

---

# 1. Core Product Shift

Lumira is moving away from:

* rigid workflow progression,
* symbolic interpretation,
* fixed direction-locking,
* and card-sequence dream processing.

Toward:

# reflective dream-space cognition.

The system should:

* hold attention,
* preserve continuity,
* support reflection,
* and deepen exploration gradually.

---

# 2. Observation Layer Redesign

## Observation should remain descriptive

Observation answers:

# “What appears in the dream?”

NOT:

# “What does the dream mean?”

Observation should become:

* richer,
* more structured,
* more phenomenological,
* more continuity-aware.

---

## 2.1 Research foundations

Primary foundations:

### Hall–Van de Castle

For:

* actors
* interactions
* aggression/friendliness
* success/failure
* social dynamics
* agency
* activity structures

### Dream phenomenology

For:

* perspective
* embodiment
* fragmentation
* continuity
* spatial logic
* narrative coherence
* memory stability
* dream-state qualities

### Lucid/metacognitive research

For:

* awareness shifts
* reflective moments
* intentionality
* dream-state recognition
* decision capacity

### Affect/emotion research

For:

* emotional transitions
* affect density
* bodily-affect relations
* unresolved emotional carryover

---

## 2.2 Observation output direction

Observation may eventually include:

* scenes
* actors
* locations
* objects
* interactions
* agency states
* affect transitions
* bodily states
* metacognitive moments
* dream-state qualities
* recurrence candidates
* continuity hints

BUT:
still descriptive and evidence-linked.

No authoritative meaning generation.

---

# 3. Latent Layer Redesign

## Latent is allowed to be interpretive internally

This is a major clarified direction.

Lumira should not claim:

* “no interpretation exists.”

Instead:

Internal cognition may include:

* probabilistic hypotheses
* recurrence inference
* tension modeling
* emotional prioritization
* agency/conflict analysis
* continuity modeling

BUT:
external communication remains:

* non-authoritative
* revisable
* optional
* user-led

---

## 3.1 Latent model foundations

### Continuity Hypothesis

For:

* cross-session patterns
* recurring concerns
* emotional continuity
* recurring relational dynamics

### Cognitive-affective dream models

For:

* unresolved tensions
* affect regulation
* avoidance loops
* emotional priorities

### Threat Simulation Theory

For:

* agency under pressure
* escape/failure patterns
* social threat dynamics
* fight/flight/freeze tendencies

### Narrative identity / self-model theory

For:

* recurring self-positions
* role structures
* agency transitions
* implicit narrative identity

### Attachment / relational dream research

For:

* closeness/distance
* authority dynamics
* abandonment
* care/dependence
* relational tension patterns

---

## 3.2 Latent output direction

Latent should produce:

* weighted reflective hypotheses
* not final conclusions

Example structure:

```json
{
  \"hypothesis\": \"Recurring blocked agency after authority encounters\",
  \"confidence\": 0.41,
  \"evidence\": [...],
  \"alternative_readings\": [...],
  \"stability\": \"emerging\"
}
```

These are:

* internal reflective navigation structures
* not user-facing truths.

---

# 4. Reflective Dialogue Layer

This layer translates:

* internal cognition
  into:
* safe reflective invitations.

The user-facing layer should:

* preserve ambiguity
* preserve agency
* avoid closure
* avoid diagnosis
* avoid symbolic certainty

Good:
“Something about this scene may relate to the earlier dream where you also felt unable to respond.”

Bad:
“This dream proves you fear authority.”

---

# 5. Highlights Redesign Direction

Highlights are becoming one of Lumira’s most important systems.

Why:
because highlights are:

* user-owned salience,
* personal attention shaping,
* continuity signals,
* meaning anchors.

Highlights may become:

# the primary interaction primitive.

---

## 5.1 Highlight roles

Highlights may support:

* notes
* recurrence memory
* glossary promotion
* reflective questions
* continuation threads
* future resurfacing
* motif relationships

---

## 5.2 Highlight philosophy

Highlights are:

* not AI-decided meaning,
* but user-marked importance.

This is crucial for:

* autonomy
* reflective ownership
* non-authoritative operation

---

# 6. Glossary Redesign Direction

Glossary is:

# personal dream memory.

NOT:

# symbolic interpretation database.

---

## 6.1 Glossary purpose

Glossary should:

1. recognize recurrence
2. preserve continuity
3. store user-associated meaning

Without:

* claiming universal interpretation.

---

## 6.2 Motif categories

Glossary may include:

* people
* places
* objects
* creatures
* dream events
* experiential states
* relational patterns

Examples:

* “teeth falling out”
* “old school”
* “cannot speak”
* “someone is watching”
* “being late”
* “endless hotel”

---

## 6.3 Candidate vs pinned

### Candidate

System notices recurrence.

### Pinned

User confirms:
“This matters enough to track.”

Promotion should remain:

* user-confirmed,
* not automatic.

---

## 6.4 Glossary behavior rule

Glossary may contextualize the dream.

Glossary may NEVER override the current dream.

Correct:
“You previously associated trains with transition. Does that resonate here too?”

Incorrect:
“The train means transition again.”

---

# 7. Work / Reflective Question Redesign

## Current problem

Current work system risks:

* rigid progression
* direction locking
* question exhaustion
* workflow feeling

---

## 7.1 Future direction

Work should become:

* reflective activation,
* thread-based,
* contextual,
* continuity-aware,
* highlight-driven,
* softer and more adaptive.

---

## 7.2 AI questions

Questions should:

* emerge from meaningful material
* feel optional
* feel contextual
* avoid interrogation feeling

Questions may attach to:

* highlights
* motifs
* saved answers
* unresolved scenes
* emotional moments
* recurrence

---

## 7.3 Direction redesign

Directions should evolve from:

* locked modes
  toward:
* attention lenses / weighting systems.

Directions may influence:

* what kinds of questions appear
* which motifs are surfaced
* which continuities are emphasized

But:
they should not rigidly constrain the system.

---

# 8. Reflective Thread Model

Long-term, work may evolve into:

# reflective threads.

A thread can originate from:

* a highlight
* a motif
* a question
* a previous answer
* a recurring pattern
* an unresolved scene

Threads can:

* remain open
* be revisited
* fade away
* reconnect later

This is closer to:

* real reflective continuity
  than:
* sequential questionnaires.

---

# 9. AI Question Philosophy

AI questions are:

# attention invitations,

not:

# required tasks.

The system should prefer:

* fewer,
* stronger,
* calmer questions.

The dream-space should remain spacious.

The AI should also know:
when not to ask.

---

# 10. Strategic Long-Term Direction

Observation:

* richer descriptive structure

Latent:

* probabilistic reflective cognition

Highlights:

* user-owned salience layer

Glossary:

* personal recurrence memory

Work:

* reflective thread system

AI:

* flow shaping through attention weighting

UX:

* reflective dream-space instead of workflow engine

This direction should be treated as the current conceptual north star for Lumira redesign.
