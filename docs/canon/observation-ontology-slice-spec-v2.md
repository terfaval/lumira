# Observation Ontology Slice Spec v2

## Affect Transitions + Emotional Contradiction

## Status

Slice Specification Draft - B-Level Observation Expansion

This document defines the second controlled B-level ontology expansion for the Observation layer.

Scope is intentionally narrow.

This slice introduces:

- affect transitions
- emotional contradiction
- affective density
- atmospheric emotional structure

as explicit phenomenological Observation dimensions.

This document is:

- implementation-guiding
- boundary-defining
- runtime-aligned

It is NOT:

- latent redesign
- psychological modeling
- therapeutic interpretation

## Purpose

Improve Lumira's descriptive modeling of emotional movement, instability, contradiction, and atmosphere without interpretive drift.

Observation should better notice how emotional states:

- emerge
- shift
- intensify
- weaken
- conflict
- remain unresolved

without claiming hidden causes or diagnostic truth.

## Slice Scope

This slice includes only:

- new first-class categories:
  - `affect_transition`
  - `emotional_contradiction`
  - `affective_atmosphere`
- bounded extraction support
- semantic validation rules
- evidence/provenance integration
- uncertainty-aware handling
- latent-safe downstream seams

## First-Class Category Decision

These dimensions are explicit categories, not generic mood tags or latent-only overlays.

Reasoning:

- emotional movement is structurally important in dream phenomenology
- continuity modeling benefits from explicit affect structure
- ambiguity preservation is easier with bounded descriptive categories

## Affect Transition

Affect transitions describe how emotional tone changes locally in the dream.

Allowed examples:

- unease becoming fear
- curiosity intensifying into panic
- calmness shifting into urgency
- fear softening into relief
- sudden emotional reversal
- slowly growing unease

This remains descriptive, not explanatory.

## Emotional Contradiction

Emotional contradiction describes simultaneous or unstable emotional coexistence.

Allowed examples:

- fear mixed with curiosity
- comfort mixed with unease
- emotional ambivalence
- emotional mismatch with scene tone
- reversal loops

Ambiguity should be preserved, not normalized away.

## Affective Atmosphere

Affective atmosphere captures emotionally charged environmental feeling.

Allowed examples:

- diffuse tension
- oppressive atmosphere
- emotional heaviness
- emotionally charged silence
- unstable ambient unease

This does not permit hidden-cause interpretation.

## Forbidden Affect Semantics

Observation must reject psychological authority claims such as:

- "the dream reveals abandonment trauma"
- "the dreamer suppresses anger"
- "the subconscious expresses insecurity"

If such hypotheses exist at all, they belong to latent probabilistic cognition, not durable Observation.

## Extraction Philosophy

Extraction remains uncertainty-first:

- preserve mixed affect
- preserve unresolvedness
- preserve instability
- prefer partial structure over unsupported coherence

Avoid:

- forced polarity
- oversimplified mood labels
- inferred emotional explanation

## Evidence and Summary Rules

Affective observations should preserve:

- supporting fragment
- local context
- uncertainty markers
- evidence adequacy tier
- summary trace compatibility

Strong affect claims require explicit or strongly implied evidence.
Weak evidence should degrade confidence or be omitted.

## Latent Interaction Rules

Latent may consume affect structures probabilistically, but must never:

- overwrite durable Observation records
- strengthen durable certainty
- convert atmosphere into psychological truth

Directionality remains:

`Observation -> Latent`

## Reflective-Space Surfacing Rules

These categories are primarily substrate-facing.

Safe:

- gentle reflective openings
- optional continuity cues
- bounded atmospheric orientation language

Unsafe:

- emotional analytics dashboards
- diagnostic summaries
- scoring interfaces

## Persistence Philosophy

Durable:

- affect transitions
- emotional contradiction observations
- affective atmosphere observations
- evidence linkage
- uncertainty metadata

Ephemeral:

- affect weighting
- emotional salience ranking
- latent emotional clustering
- interpretive overlays

## Migration Philosophy

Keep implementation:

- additive
- backward-compatible
- rollback-safe
- thin

Avoid:

- emotional graph over-engineering
- rigid polarity systems
- broad latent coupling

## Validation Expectations

Validate:

- mixed-affect allowed cases
- interpretive rejection cases
- ambiguity preservation
- contradiction handling
- evidence degradation behavior
- provenance continuity
- latent backflow prevention

## Sequencing

This slice should come before:

- spatial instability enrichment
- phenomenological relation modeling
- deeper continuity cognition

## Final Principle

This slice succeeds when Lumira better notices emotional movement, contradiction, and atmosphere without pretending to know why those emotions ultimately exist.
