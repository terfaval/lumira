# LUMIRA BACKEND V2 CANON

## Status

Canonical architecture document.

This document defines the purpose, structure, boundaries and information flow of the Lumira Backend V2 architecture.

All layer-specific documents derive from this canon.

When conflicts emerge between implementation details, historical documents or future proposals, this document serves as the primary architectural reference.

---

# 1. Foundational Premise

Lumira is founded on a simple premise:

> Dreams are one of the richest available paths toward understanding the unconscious aspects of human experience.

Lumira does not attempt to replace that process.

Lumira exists as a companion within that process.

The system does not seek to explain dreams on behalf of the user.

The system seeks to create conditions in which understanding may emerge.

Meaning belongs to the user.

Reflection is valued above interpretation.

Exploration is valued above certainty.

Questions are often more valuable than answers.

---

# 2. What Lumira Is

Lumira is not:

* a dream interpreter,
* a symbolic dictionary,
* a psychological diagnosis system,
* a therapeutic authority,
* a truth engine.

Lumira is a reflective cognition system.

Its purpose is to:

* preserve dream experience,
* organize continuity,
* identify reflective possibilities,
* support reflective exploration,
* accumulate understanding,
* create navigable representations of dream life.

Lumira helps users build an ongoing relationship with their dreams.

# 5. Canonical Information Hierarchy

Not all information carries equal authority.

Lumira prioritizes information according to evidential grounding.

Highest authority:

Dream Material

↓

Observation

↓

Glossary Continuity

↓

Reflections

Lowest authority:

Latent Possibilities

---

## Evidence Hierarchy

### Dream Material

Direct user-reported experience.

Highest evidential status.

---

### Observation

Structured descriptions derived from dream material.

Must remain traceable to dream evidence.

---

### Glossary

Continuity selected and preserved by the user.

Represents recurring dream material and personal relevance.

---

### Reflections

User-derived understanding.

Represents the strongest form of personal meaning within the system.

---

### Latent

Reflective possibilities.

Latent may suggest.

Latent may never override evidence.

Latent may never override user understanding.

# 10. Long-Term Vision

Over time Lumira becomes:

* a memory of dreams,
* a memory of continuity,
* a memory of understanding,
* a map of recurring experience,
* a companion for reflective exploration.

The system should become increasingly grounded.

Not because it becomes more certain.

But because it becomes increasingly connected to:

* dream evidence,
* continuity,
* personal reflection,
* accumulated understanding.

Lumira is not designed to tell users what their dreams mean.

Lumira is designed to help users develop a deeper relationship with their dream life and, through that relationship, with themselves.

---

# 11. Canonical Principle

If a future design decision creates tension between:

* explanation and exploration,
* certainty and ambiguity,
* interpretation and reflection,
* system authority and user agency,

the architecture should favor:

* exploration,
* ambiguity,
* reflection,
* and user ownership of meaning.

This principle supersedes all implementation details.
