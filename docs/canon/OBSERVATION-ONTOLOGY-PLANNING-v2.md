# Observation Ontology Planning v2

## Status

Planning Draft — Canonical Observation Layer Direction

This document defines:

* the intended conceptual shape of the Observation layer,
* canonical descriptive boundaries,
* ontology scope,
* evidence discipline,
* phenomenological modeling foundations,
* extensibility strategy,
* and future expansion seams.

This document is:

* conceptual,
* architecture-guiding,
* cognition-boundary-defining.

It is NOT:

* an implementation spec,
* a DB schema,
* an extraction algorithm,
* or an API contract.

The goal is:

# establishing a stable descriptive substrate for Lumira’s reflective runtime.

---

# 1. Core Principle

Observation exists to describe:

* what appears,
* how experience unfolds,
* how experience is phenomenologically felt,
* and how local meaning manifests inside the dream itself.

Observation does NOT determine:

* ultimate meaning,
* hidden psychological truth,
* symbolic certainty,
* or final interpretation.

---

# 2. Architectural Role

Observation sits between:

```txt id="ljcl8r"
Reflective Object
    ↓
Observation
    ↓
Latent Cognition
    ↓
Reflective Openings / Threads / Orientation
```

Observation provides:

# structured phenomenological orientation substrate.

It does not:

# own reflective truth.

---

# 3. Observation Philosophy

Observation should not become:

* sterile event parsing,
* NLP sludge,
* symbolic decoding,
* hidden interpretation,
* or synthetic coherence generation.

The goal is:

# structured phenomenology.

The Observation layer should preserve:

* experiential texture,
* emotional atmosphere,
* ambiguity,
* instability,
* fragmentation,
* dream logic,
* and local meaning structures

WITHOUT:

* hallucinating certainty,
* inventing unsupported structures,
* or imposing interpretive narratives.

---

# 4. Current Planning Target

The current target is:

# Medium-Rich Observation (B-level)

This level should provide enough descriptive richness to support:

* reflective orientation,
* continuity-sensitive cognition,
* thread emergence,
* glossary continuity,
* latent grounding,
* and future cognition expansion,

WITHOUT:

* collapsing into interpretation,
* over-taxonomizing experience,
* or becoming psychologically authoritative.

# 5. Research Foundations

The Observation layer is intentionally multi-foundational.

No single research model fully captures dream phenomenology or reflective continuity.

---

## 5.1 Hall–Van de Castle (Core Structural Foundation)

Hall–Van de Castle is treated as:

# the primary structural observation foundation.

Useful for:

* actors
* interactions
* aggression/friendliness
* success/failure
* social structure
* agency dynamics
* relational positioning
* interaction asymmetry
* activity structures

Why it fits Lumira:

* descriptive rather than symbolic
* structured but not overly interpretive
* phenomenologically grounded
* continuity-compatible
* emotionally relevant without claiming certainty

HvC should strongly influence:

* actor ontology
* interaction ontology
* agency structures
* relational structures
* social continuity modeling

---

## 5.2 Dream Phenomenology Research

Dream phenomenology is treated as:

# a foundational experiential layer.

Useful for:

* fragmentation
* instability
* dream logic
* impossible transitions
* spatial discontinuity
* altered realism
* identity instability
* false awakening
* continuity disruption
* dream-state texture

This layer is critical because:

dreams are not only narrative structures,
but altered experiential environments.

This dimension helps prevent Observation from collapsing into:

# mere event extraction.

---

## 5.3 Affect and Emotional Dynamics Research

Emotion modeling is treated as:

# phenomenological affect structure.

Useful for:

* emotional transitions
* affect escalation/de-escalation
* emotional ambiguity
* atmospheric tension
* diffuse unease
* emotionally dense moments
* conflicting emotional states
* affective drift

Important:

Emotion modeling should preserve:

* ambiguity,
* contradiction,
* instability,
* and emotional texture.

Observation should avoid reducing emotion to:

# simplistic mood tagging.

---

## 5.4 Lucid / Metacognitive Dream Research

Metacognitive dream research supports:

# reflective-awareness observation.

Useful for:

* awareness shifts
* noticing dream strangeness
* reflective moments
* reality questioning
* intentional dream action
* dream-state recognition
* deliberate decisions
* self-awareness changes

This dimension is important because:

Lumira is fundamentally a reflective system.

Metacognitive moments are therefore legitimate descriptive dream structures.

---

## 5.5 Continuity-Aware Descriptive Modeling

Observation may preserve:

* recurrence candidates
* repeated atmospheres
* repeated interaction structures
* repeated dream-state qualities
* repeated emotional configurations

BUT:

Observation must not collapse recurrence into interpretation.

Correct:

* “similar interaction pattern appeared in multiple dreams”

Incorrect:

* “this recurring pattern proves unresolved authority conflict”

# 12. Descriptive vs Interpretive Boundary

This boundary is critical.

---

## Descriptive (allowed)

Examples:

* “the scarecrow produced fear in the dreamer”
* “the authority figure repeatedly interrupted speech”
* “the emotional tone shifted from curiosity to fear”
* “the dreamer felt trapped during pursuit”
* “the environment became increasingly unstable”

---

## Interpretive (not allowed in Observation)

Examples:

* “fear of authority”
* “suppressed trauma”
* “attachment insecurity”
* “identity fragmentation”
* “fear of abandonment”
* “the scarecrow represents the father”

These belong, if anywhere, to:

# latent probabilistic cognition.

Never canonical Observation truth.

---

Observation persistence should remain:

# restrained.

* scenes
