# Minimal Reflective Runtime v1 (Draft)

## Status

Draft — Clean-room Runtime Foundation

This document defines:

- the smallest meaningful Lumira runtime,
- the minimum canonical reflective topology,
- foundational runtime primitives,
- minimum reflective movement rules,
- and explicit scope boundaries for the first clean-room rebuild.

This is not:
- a schema specification,
- a database design,
- a UI implementation plan,
- or a feature-complete system vision.

The goal is not completeness.

The goal is:
# the first stable Lumira organism.

---

# 1. Purpose of the Minimal Runtime

The minimal runtime exists to support:

- reflective orientation,
- continuity awareness,
- dream-connected self-reflection,
- symbolic exploration,
- and emotionally sustainable reflective movement.

The runtime must remain:
- calm,
- breathable,
- non-coercive,
- continuity-aware,
- and cognitively restrained.

The first rebuild must optimize for:
- clarity,
- integrity,
- coherence,
- and emotional safety.

Not feature breadth.

---

# 2. Foundational Shift

Lumira is not fundamentally organized around sessions.

Sessions may exist as temporary interaction containers.

But the primary ontology is:

# Reflective Space

Reflective Space is the living reflective environment within which continuity can emerge:
- dreams,
- threads,
- motifs,
- reflections,
- openings,
- and symbolic associations
exist and evolve.

Dreams are privileged reflective anchors.

But dreams are not the only legitimate reflective objects.

---

# 3. Core Runtime Topology

The minimal runtime topology is:

```txt
Reflective Space
    ├── Dream Entries
    ├── Observation Layer
    ├── Latent Cognition Layer
    ├── Reflective Threads
    └── Glossary Memory
````

Everything else is secondary or future-stage.

---

# 4. Canonical Runtime Primitives

The first clean-room rebuild should treat the following as canonical primitives.

---

## 4.1 Dream Entry

Dream Entry is the primary experiential substrate.

Dream entries represent:

* remembered dream experiences,
* dream fragments,
* scenes,
* emotional moments,
* symbolic impressions,
* or partially remembered dream traces.

Dream entries are not:

* tasks,
* workflow stages,
* or isolated containers.

A dream may:

* connect to multiple threads,
* relate to glossary elements,
* generate multiple observations,
* or remain largely unresolved.

Multiple dreams may also contribute to the same reflective continuity.

---

## 4.2 Observation Layer

Observation is the descriptive orientation layer.

Observation answers:

# “What appears here?”

not:

# “What does this mean?”

Observation may include:

* scenes,
* actors,
* interactions,
* emotional transitions,
* agency states,
* spatial structures,
* dream-state qualities,
* relational dynamics,
* phenomenological characteristics,
* and recurrence candidates.

Observation should remain:

* evidence-linked,
* descriptive,
* structured,
* and non-authoritative.

Observation provides orientation structure without collapsing ambiguity.

Research-informed descriptive systems such as:

* Hall–Van de Castle,
* phenomenological dream models,
* and metacognitive dream research
  may inform the layer.

But observation remains reflective scaffolding, not interpretive truth.

---

## 4.3 Latent Cognition Layer

Latent cognition is the runtime’s internal reflective thinking layer.

Latent cognition may:

* model continuity,
* detect recurrence,
* identify tensions,
* infer relational dynamics,
* estimate salience,
* track unresolved patterns,
* identify possible connections,
* and detect reflective opportunities.

Latent cognition is:

# internal cognition.

It is not surfaced truth.

The latent layer exists:

* to support orientation,
* improve reflective sensitivity,
* and shape reflective invitations.

Not to generate authoritative interpretation.

Latent cognition must remain:

* probabilistic,
* revisable,
* uncertainty-aware,
* and subordinate to user meaning.

---

## 4.4 Reflective Threads

Reflective threads are revisitable reflective trajectories.

Threads are not:

* tasks,
* workflows,
* progression tracks,
* or mandatory journeys.

Threads are living reflective structures that can participate in continuity through the wider ecology.

A thread may:

* emerge,
* deepen,
* branch,
* reconnect,
* remain dormant,
* fade,
* or reappear later.

Threads may connect:

* multiple dreams,
* glossary motifs,
* emotional dynamics,
* recurring situations,
* questions,
* reflective realizations,
* or unresolved tensions.

Threads are navigational and orientational structures.

Not completion systems.

---

## 4.5 Glossary Memory

Glossary memory is the system’s symbolic continuity memory.

The glossary preserves:

* recurring motifs,
* personal symbolic associations,
* emotional resonance,
* user-defined meanings,
* recurring people/places/objects,
* and continuity context.

The glossary is:

# autobiographical symbolic memory.

Not:

* a universal symbol dictionary,
* or a fixed interpretation database.

Glossary associations must remain:

* contextual,
* revisable,
* and user-owned.

The glossary may contextualize reflective movement.

It may never override present experience.

---

# 5. Reflective Movement Model

The minimal runtime supports:

# navigable reflective movement.

Not:

* linear progression,
* or forced processing sequences.

Reflective movement may include:

* deepening,
* revisiting,
* lateral association,
* resurfacing,
* contextual widening,
* symbolic linking,
* or temporary withdrawal.

The system should support:

* moving between dreams,
* returning to earlier material,
* connecting motifs,
* following threads,
* or remaining with unresolved material.

Movement is more important than completion.

Orientation is more important than progression.

---

# 6. Reflective Openings

Reflective openings exist in the minimal runtime.

However:
they are secondary primitives.

Openings are:

* optional reflective invitations,
* not mandatory workflow actions.

The runtime should prefer:

* fewer,
* calmer,
* contextually resonant openings.

Openings should emerge from:

* meaningful continuity,
* reflective salience,
* or emotionally legible tension.

The system must know when not to ask.

Silence is a valid runtime outcome.

---

# 7. The Role of Sessions

Sessions are downgraded from primary ontology.

A session may exist as:

* a temporary interaction window,
* a reflective checkpoint,
* or a bounded working context.

But:

* continuity transcends sessions,
* threads transcend sessions,
* glossary memory transcends sessions,
* and reflective movement transcends sessions.

The runtime must not trap continuity inside isolated session containers.

---

# 8. Runtime Gravity

The runtime’s primary gravitational center is:

# reflective orientation.

Not:

* completion,
* insight extraction,
* productivity,
* or workflow progression.

The runtime exists to help the user:

* remain oriented,
* gradually clarify inner continuity,
* sustain reflective contact,
* and navigate symbolic material safely.

---

# 9. Emotional and Cognitive Pacing

The runtime must remain:

* emotionally breathable,
* cognitively spacious,
* and low-pressure.

The runtime must avoid:

* continuity flooding,
* over-connection,
* interpretive overload,
* compulsive resurfacing,
* endless questioning,
* or synthetic coherence generation.

Under ambiguity:

* omission is preferred over forced surfacing.

---

# 10. Minimal Runtime Constraints

The first rebuild should explicitly avoid:

* complex graph visualizations
* large-scale continuity orchestration
* aggressive resurfacing systems
* multi-agent cognition systems
* deep symbolic scoring systems
* “AI insight feed” behavior
* compulsive recommendation systems
* gamification
* streak systems
* productivity framing
* completion metrics
* authority language
* dense reflective dashboards

The minimal runtime should remain:

* quiet,
* restrained,
* and structurally understandable.

---

# 11. First Rebuild Scope Philosophy

The first rebuild should prioritize:

* stable ontology,
* clean continuity structures,
* reflective integrity,
* and architectural clarity.

The goal is not:

* maximum intelligence,
* maximum automation,
* or maximum connection density.

The goal is:

# a trustworthy reflective organism.

A smaller but coherent Lumira is preferable to a larger but psychologically unstable one.

---

# 12. Final Principle

The minimal runtime should feel like:

* entering a calm reflective continuity space,
* not operating a cognitive machine.

The system should help the user:

* notice,
* orient,
* connect,
* revisit,
* and gradually clarify inner material.

Without pressure.

Without certainty.

Without losing ambiguity.

The runtime should remain spacious enough for the unconscious to remain alive.
