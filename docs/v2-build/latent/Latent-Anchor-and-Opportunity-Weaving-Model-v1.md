# Purpose

This document defines:

* the Latent Anchor primitive
* Anchor categories
* Anchor identity and manifestation
* Anchor relationships
* Opportunity Weaving
* reflective network formation

The purpose of this document is to establish how reflective continuity is organized within Backend V2.

This document does not define:

* Opening generation
* Thread implementation
* Reflection implementation
* database schema
* scoring systems
* implementation details

---

# Core Principle

The Latent layer organizes reflective continuity through Latent Anchors.

Anchors are not Opportunities.

Anchors are not Threads.

Anchors are not Reflections.

Anchors are connective reflective primitives that allow dreams, Opportunities, Threads, and Reflections to participate in larger reflective networks.

---

# Why Anchors Exist

Backend V2 contains multiple reflective primitives:

* Dreams
* Observations
* Glossary Entries
* Opportunities
* Threads
* Reflections

These primitives alone do not explain why separate reflective elements may belong to the same larger reflective landscape.

Latent Anchors exist to provide connective structure.

Their purpose is not interpretation.

Their purpose is organization.

---

# Latent Anchor

A Latent Anchor is a reflective connection primitive.

An Anchor identifies a recurring or meaningful point of reflective organization.

Anchors may emerge from:

* dream material
* Opportunity construction
* Thread activity
* Reflection activity

Anchors may later participate in reflective continuity and reflective network formation.

An Anchor does not require existing continuity.

An Anchor may emerge from a single dream.

---

# Non-Interpretive Anchor Principle

Anchors should remain non-interpretive.

Anchors may represent:

* entities
* roles
* reflective structures

Anchors should not represent:

* conclusions
* psychological explanations
* diagnoses
* interpretations
* resolved meaning

Examples:

Valid:

```text id="e2dbqw"
Father
Guide
Witness
Threat
Exploration → Danger
Protection ↔ Threat
```

Not Valid:

```text id="7zj0uh"
Authority Issues
Fear of Abandonment
Need for Validation
Difficulty Trusting Others
```

Such constructs belong to later reflective layers.

---

# Anchor Categories

Backend V2 recognizes three primary Anchor categories.

---

## Entity Anchors

Entity Anchors represent identifiable dream elements.

Examples:

```text id="g0z66k"
Father
House
River
Dog
Mountain
```

Entity Anchors describe what appears.

They do not describe function or meaning.

---

## Role Anchors

Role Anchors represent functional positions within reflective material.

Examples:

```text id="n0qf0n"
Guide
Protector
Threat
Witness
Companion
Follower
Authority
```

Role Anchors describe what a dream element is doing.

They do not describe who or what the element is.

---

## Structural Anchors

Structural Anchors represent recurring reflective structures.

Examples:

```text id="wlg8pi"
Exploration → Danger

Protection ↔ Threat

Approach ↔ Withdrawal

Belonging ↔ Exclusion
```

Structural Anchors describe relationships and dynamics.

They do not describe specific dream content.

---

# Category Independence Principle

An Anchor does not require membership in multiple categories.

An Anchor may exist solely as:

* an Entity Anchor
* a Role Anchor
* a Structural Anchor

Cross-category participation is allowed but not required.

---

# Anchor Identity

Anchor Identities represent stable reflective organizing points.

The purpose of an Anchor Identity is to prevent unnecessary Anchor proliferation.

Anchor Identities should remain relatively stable over time.

Examples:

```text id="k9gjvr"
Guide
```

```text id="k0i6lf"
Exploration → Danger
```

These represent Anchor Identities.

---

# Anchor Manifestations

Anchor Manifestations represent concrete appearances of an Anchor Identity.

Examples:

Anchor Identity:

```text id="lt1jvs"
Guide
```

Manifestations:

```text id="5mmdga"
Father as Guide

Teacher as Guide

Unknown Figure as Guide
```

Anchor Identity:

```text id="yn8j0o"
Exploration → Danger
```

Manifestations:

```text id="z3f7z4"
Journey → Threat

Building Exploration → Confinement

Mountain Climb → Collapse
```

The Identity remains stable.

Manifestations vary.

---

# Anchor Normalization Principle

The Latent layer should prefer reuse of existing Anchor Identities when meaningful equivalence exists.

Anchor proliferation should be minimized.

New Anchor Identities should be created only when existing Anchor Identities cannot credibly represent the observed material.

The purpose of normalization is continuity.

Not reduction.

---

# Anchor Creation Principle

Anchors may be created by:

* Latent processing
* Reflection processing

Both system-derived and user-derived reflective knowledge may contribute to Anchor formation.

Reflection-derived Anchors should be treated as especially significant forms of reflective evidence.

---

# Anchor Abundance Principle

Reflective structures are overlapping rather than exclusive.

A single:

* Dream
* Opportunity
* Thread
* Reflection

may participate in many Anchors simultaneously.

The system should not assume a single dominant Anchor.

Multiple concurrent Anchors are expected.

---

# Anchor Relationships

Anchors may relate to other Anchors.

Anchor relationships may emerge through:

* repeated co-occurrence
* shared Opportunities
* shared Threads
* shared Reflections
* recurring reflective proximity

Examples:

```text id="mbrnvi"
Guide
↔
Following Another
```

```text id="6gbjjj"
Protection ↔ Threat
↔
Trust ↔ Danger
```

Anchor relationships should remain evidence-based.

Relationship creation should remain conservative.

---

# Anchor–Opportunity Orthogonality Principle

Anchors and Opportunities are distinct reflective primitives.

Neither primitive owns the other.

Anchors may exist without associated Opportunities.

Opportunities may exist without associated Anchors.

Anchors and Opportunities may interact, reinforce one another, and participate in shared reflective structures.

However:

Anchors should not be treated as Opportunity containers.

Opportunities should not be treated as Anchor instances.

The purpose of Anchors is reflective organization.

The purpose of Opportunities is reflective possibility.

The distinction should remain visible throughout the system.

---

# Opportunity Weaving

Opportunity Weaving is the process through which Opportunities become connected through shared Anchor participation.

The primary unit of Weaving is not the Opportunity.

The primary unit of Weaving is the Anchor.

Conceptually:

```text id="pjx5wr"
Opportunity A
↓
Anchor
↓
Opportunity B
```

Shared Anchor participation creates opportunities for reflective continuity.

---

# Thread Weaving

Threads may also become connected through shared Anchor participation.

Conceptually:

```text id="0f6m5x"
Thread A
↓
Anchor
↓
Thread B
```

Thread relationships should emerge through reflective evidence rather than forced continuation.

---

# Reflection Weaving

Reflections may contribute new Anchors and strengthen existing Anchor relationships.

Reflections therefore participate in Weaving both as evidence sources and as network participants.

Reflection-derived Weaving should be treated as highly significant.

---

# Reflective Network Formation

Reflective networks emerge from the interaction of:

* Anchors
* Opportunities
* Threads
* Reflections

Conceptually:

```text id="oj1x3j"
Anchors
↓
Opportunities
↓
Threads
↓
Reflections
```

and simultaneously:

```text id="m6yvc0"
Reflections
↓
Threads
↓
Opportunities
↓
Anchors
```

Reflective networks are not strictly hierarchical.

They are evolving systems of reflective association.

---

# Continuity Without Conflation Principle

Weaving exists to support continuity.

Weaving does not justify collapsing distinct reflective structures into one.

The system should preserve:

* dream integrity
* Opportunity integrity
* Thread integrity
* Reflection integrity

even when strong Anchor relationships exist.

Continuity should never erase distinction.

---

# Final Principle

Latent Anchors provide the connective fabric of Backend V2.

They allow reflective continuity to emerge without forcing interpretation.

They allow reflective networks to grow without collapsing distinct experiences into a single narrative.

The purpose of Weaving is not to explain the user's experience.

The purpose of Weaving is to preserve meaningful pathways through it.
