# Purpose

This document defines how Reflective Opportunities persist, change, and evolve over time.

The purpose of this document is to establish:

* Opportunity Identity
* Opportunity Manifestation
* Opportunity lifecycle events
* Opportunity updates
* Opportunity interaction with Threads and Reflections

This document does not define:

* Opportunity construction
* Opening generation
* Thread implementation
* scoring systems
* implementation details

---

# Core Principle

Reflective Opportunities are not static records.

They are persistent reflective structures that may evolve through interaction with dreams, Threads, and Reflections.

The purpose of the lifecycle is not to preserve certainty.

The purpose of the lifecycle is to preserve reflective continuity while remaining open to revision.

---

# Opportunity Identity

An Opportunity Identity represents a persistent reflective structure.

It is not:

* a dream
* an Observation
* a Glossary Entity
* a Thread
* a Reflection

An Opportunity Identity may persist across multiple dreams.

An Opportunity Identity should not be treated as a fixed interpretation.

It remains open to change.

---

# Opportunity Manifestation

An Opportunity Manifestation is a dream-specific appearance of an Opportunity Identity.

A manifestation is anchored to a specific Priority Dream.

Multiple manifestations may contribute to the same Opportunity Identity.

The Opportunity Identity is not equivalent to any single manifestation.

---

# Identity Preservation Principle

The Latent layer should prefer preservation of an existing Opportunity Identity when meaningful reflective continuity remains credible.

New Opportunity Identities should be created when meaningful continuity can no longer be established.

Identity preservation should remain conservative.

Continuity should not be assumed automatically.

---

# Emergence

An Opportunity Identity emerges when the Latent layer identifies a sufficiently credible reflective structure.

Initial emergence may occur from:

* Dream-Originated Opportunities
* Context-Revealed Opportunities

Every Opportunity Identity must remain anchored to a current dream.

---

# Reinforcement

An Opportunity Identity may be reinforced.

Reinforcement occurs when additional manifestations contribute supporting reflective evidence.

Reinforcement increases confidence that the reflective structure remains relevant.

Reinforcement does not imply correctness.

---

# Expansion

An Opportunity Identity may expand.

Expansion occurs when new manifestations reveal additional dimensions of an existing reflective structure.

Expansion increases reflective scope without replacing identity.

---

# Recontextualization

An Opportunity Identity may be recontextualized.

Recontextualization occurs when new manifestations place an existing reflective structure into a different reflective context.

The underlying Opportunity Identity may remain intact.

The surrounding understanding changes.

---

# Reversal

An Opportunity Identity may be challenged or reversed by later manifestations.

Examples include:

```text
Protection ↔ Danger
```

```text
Belonging ↔ Exclusion
```

```text
Trust ↔ Threat
```

A reversal does not necessarily create a new Opportunity Identity.

Reversal may reveal additional dimensions of the existing reflective structure.

---

# Weakening

An Opportunity Identity may weaken.

Weakening may occur when:

* supporting manifestations stop appearing
* reflective exploration reduces relevance
* newer Opportunities become more central

Weakening does not imply failure.

Weakening does not imply incorrectness.

---

# Reactivation

An Opportunity Identity may reactivate.

Reactivation occurs when a previously weakened Opportunity receives meaningful new support.

Reactivation may occur through:

* new dreams
* new reflections
* new continuity information
* new Thread activity

Reactivation should be treated as a distinct lifecycle event.

---

# Splitting

An Opportunity Identity may split.

Splitting occurs when reflective exploration reveals that a previously unified structure actually contains multiple distinct reflective structures.

The resulting Opportunity Identities may continue independently.

---

# Merging

Multiple Opportunity Identities may merge.

Merging occurs when reflective exploration reveals meaningful continuity between previously separate reflective structures.

Merging should remain conservative.

Artificial consolidation should be avoided.

---

# Abandonment

An Opportunity Identity may eventually be abandoned.

Abandonment occurs when the system no longer considers the Opportunity Identity meaningfully useful for future reflective work.

Abandonment does not imply error.

It indicates loss of reflective relevance.

The system should be conservative when abandoning Opportunity Identities.

---

# Thread Influence

Threads may influence Opportunity lifecycle.

Thread activity may:

* reinforce Opportunities
* weaken Opportunities
* recontextualize Opportunities
* reactivate Opportunities
* reveal new Opportunities
* support splitting
* support merging

Threads are active participants in Opportunity evolution.

---

# Reflection Influence

Reflections may influence Opportunity lifecycle.

Reflections provide the strongest form of user-generated reflective evidence available to the system.

Reflection material may:

* reinforce Opportunities
* challenge Opportunities
* recontextualize Opportunities
* reactivate Opportunities

Reflection influence should be treated as highly significant.

---

# Update Principle

Opportunity updates should occur when meaningful new evidence becomes available.

Examples include:

* new dreams
* new reflections
* new glossary continuity
* new Thread activity

The purpose of updating is reassessment.

The purpose is not retroactive interpretation enforcement.

---

# Stability Principle

The Latent layer should balance stability and adaptability.

Opportunity Identities should not change so rapidly that continuity becomes impossible.

Opportunity Identities should not become so rigid that revision becomes impossible.

The preferred behavior is evidence-based adaptation.

---

# Final Principle

Reflective Opportunities are intended to support long-term reflective exploration.

A healthy Opportunity Identity remains capable of:

* persistence
* revision
* reactivation
* transformation

without collapsing into certainty.

The purpose of the lifecycle is to preserve reflective continuity while remaining open to future understanding.
