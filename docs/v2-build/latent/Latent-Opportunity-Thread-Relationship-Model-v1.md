# Opportunity–Thread Relationship Model v1

## Status

Planning Canon

Backend V2 Foundation Layer

Depends on:

* Reflective Opportunity Contract v1

---

# Purpose

This document defines the relationship between Reflective Opportunities and Threads.

The purpose of this document is to establish:

* how Threads emerge
* how Opportunities and Threads interact
* how reflective exploration influences the Latent layer
* how continuity is preserved without enforcing continuation

This document does not define:

* Opening generation
* Thread implementation
* Reflection persistence
* Opportunity lifecycle
* implementation details

---

# Core Principle

Reflective Opportunities and Threads are distinct primitives.

They serve different purposes.

A Reflective Opportunity represents reflective possibility.

A Thread represents reflective exploration.

Neither primitive replaces the other.

---

# Reflective Opportunity

A Reflective Opportunity is an internal Latent-layer construct.

Its purpose is to identify potentially fruitful directions for reflection.

An Opportunity may exist without ever becoming a Thread.

An Opportunity does not require user engagement.

---

# Thread

A Thread is a user-engaged reflective process.

A Thread exists because a user chose to enter a reflective direction.

A Thread is not created by the Latent layer alone.

A Thread is not created by Opening generation alone.

A Thread emerges through reflective engagement.

---

# Thread Creation Principle

Thread creation follows the sequence:

```text
Opportunity
↓
Opening
↓
User Engagement
↓
Thread
```

An Opportunity may exist without an Opening.

An Opening may exist without a Thread.

A Thread requires user participation.

---

# Human Validation Principle

The Latent layer may identify reflective possibilities.

The user determines which possibilities become active reflective work.

Thread creation represents a form of reflective validation.

This validation does not imply correctness.

It indicates willingness to explore.

---

# Opportunity Independence Principle

Reflective Opportunities do not require Threads.

The Latent layer may identify Opportunities that are:

* never opened
* never explored
* never revisited

Such Opportunities remain valid parts of the reflective landscape.

The system should not force Opportunity activation.

---

# Thread Independence Principle

Threads do not depend on the continued existence of the Opportunity that originally contributed to them.

A Thread may continue even if:

* the originating Opportunity weakens
* the originating Opportunity evolves
* the originating Opportunity merges
* the originating Opportunity splits
* the originating Opportunity disappears

The reflective work performed within the Thread remains meaningful.

---

# Many-to-Many Relationship Principle

Opportunities and Threads form a many-to-many relationship.

An Opportunity may contribute to multiple Threads.

A Thread may engage multiple Opportunities.

Neither side owns the other.

---

# Opportunity Contribution

An Opportunity may contribute to a Thread by:

* initiating exploration
* providing reflective context
* suggesting reflective direction
* supplying continuity information

Opportunity contribution is informational.

It is not directive.

---

# Thread Contribution

A Thread may contribute to Opportunities.

Reflective exploration may:

* strengthen an Opportunity
* weaken an Opportunity
* refine an Opportunity
* clarify an Opportunity
* split an Opportunity
* merge Opportunities
* reveal new Opportunities

Threads are not passive consumers of Opportunities.

Threads actively reshape the reflective landscape.

---

# Opportunity Discovery During Reflection

New Opportunities may emerge during active Thread exploration.

A Thread is not limited to the Opportunities that initially contributed to it.

Reflective exploration may uncover:

* new tensions
* new contradictions
* new continuities
* new relationships
* new reflective directions

The Latent layer should remain open to Opportunity emergence during reflective work.

---

# Reflective Reassessment Principle

Reflections do not directly create Reflective Opportunities.

Reflections contribute new reflective evidence.

The Latent layer may reassess the reflective landscape based on that evidence.

New Opportunities may emerge through reassessment.

Opportunity ownership remains with the Latent layer.

Reflection ownership remains with the user.

The purpose of reassessment is to preserve the distinction between reflective possibility and reflective understanding.

---

# Thread Continuity

Threads may persist across multiple dreams.

A Thread may accumulate:

* multiple Opportunities
* multiple Openings
* multiple Reflections
* multiple Dream Evidence Blocks

Thread continuity reflects ongoing reflective exploration.

It does not imply interpretive certainty.

---

# Continuity Without Pressure Principle

The existence of a prior Thread must not create automatic continuation pressure.

The system may identify:

* continuity
* resonance
* development
* reappearance

The system must not assume:

* unfinished work
* unresolved processing
* obligation to continue

Continuation remains a user decision.

---

# Opportunity–Thread Network

Opportunities and Threads together form a reflective network.

Conceptually:

```text
Opportunity A
 ↘
  Thread X
 ↗

Opportunity B
```

and:

```text
Thread X
 ↘
  Opportunity C
 ↗

Thread Y
```

The purpose of the network is to preserve reflective continuity while allowing reflective freedom.

The network should remain flexible and evolvable.

---

# Reflective Ownership Principle

The Latent layer owns Opportunity construction.

The user owns reflective engagement.

Threads emerge at the boundary between the two.

Threads therefore represent shared reflective territory.

Neither purely system-generated nor purely system-controlled.

---

# Final Principle

Reflective Opportunities identify where reflection may be valuable.

Threads represent the act of reflection itself.

The relationship between them should support exploration, continuity, and discovery without enforcing conclusions, interpretations, or predetermined paths.
