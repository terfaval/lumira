# Reflective Opportunity Contract v1

## Status

Planning Canon

Backend V2 Foundation Layer

---

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical Reflective Opportunity.

The Reflective Opportunity is the foundational primitive of the Latent layer.

Its purpose is to establish:

* what a Reflective Opportunity is
* what it is not
* where it comes from
* how it relates to other Backend V2 layers

This document does not define:

* Latent processing
* Opening generation
* Thread implementation
* Reflection persistence
* database structures
* implementation details

---

# Core Principle

The Latent layer does not preserve truth.

The Latent layer does not preserve interpretation.

The Latent layer preserves reflective possibility.

The primary unit of reflective possibility is the:

```text
Reflective Opportunity
```

---

# Definition

A Reflective Opportunity is a potentially fruitful direction for reflection identified by the Latent layer.

It represents a pattern, relationship, dynamic, transition, tension, contradiction, continuity signal, or other reflective structure that may reward exploration.

A Reflective Opportunity is not:

* a conclusion
* an interpretation
* a diagnosis
* a symbolic meaning
* a truth claim

A Reflective Opportunity is a structured possibility for reflection.

---

# Current Dream Authority

The current dream is the primary authority for every Reflective Opportunity.

No Reflective Opportunity may exist without support from the current dream.

Glossary continuity, prior dreams, and prior reflections may enrich an Opportunity.

They may not replace the current dream as its foundation.

The Latent layer must remain capable of producing meaningful Opportunities from a user's very first dream.

---

# Evidence Principle

Reflective Opportunities must remain evidence-grounded.

An Opportunity should always be traceable to supporting material.

Possible supporting material includes:

```text
Observations
Scenes
Scene transitions
Glossary entities
Appearance history
Reflections
```

Reflective possibility is exploratory.

It is not arbitrary.

Opportunities should not exist solely because they sound plausible.

---

# Opportunity Shapes

A Reflective Opportunity may take many forms.

Examples include:

```text
Relationship
Dynamic
Transition
Tension
Contradiction
Recurring Pattern
Unresolved Pattern
Continuity Signal
Notable Absence
```

A Reflective Opportunity is not limited to a single element.

Many Opportunities emerge from relationships between multiple elements.

---

# Opportunity Sources

Reflective Opportunities may emerge from multiple sources.

---

## Dream-Internal Sources

Opportunities may emerge from:

* relationships between observations
* relationships between scenes
* scene transitions
* tensions within the dream
* contradictions within the dream
* repeated dream structures
* unresolved dream structures
* notable absences
* emerging dynamics

These sources require no prior continuity history.

---

## Cross-Layer Sources

Opportunities may also emerge from:

* Observation ↔ Glossary relationships
* Observation ↔ Reflection relationships
* Glossary ↔ Reflection relationships

Including:

* agreement
* contradiction
* tension
* resonance
* absence
* unresolved continuity

Cross-layer sources may strengthen or expand an Opportunity.

They do not override the current dream.

---

# Relationship To Observation

Observation answers:

```text
What happened?
```

Observation provides descriptive evidence.

Observation does not create Reflective Opportunities.

---

# Relationship To Glossary

Glossary answers:

```text
What returns?
```

Glossary provides continuity information.

Glossary does not create Reflective Opportunities.

---

# Relationship To Reflections

Reflections answer:

```text
What has been understood?
```

Reflections provide user-grounded understanding.

Reflections do not create Reflective Opportunities.

---

# Relationship To Latent

Latent answers:

```text
What may be worth exploring?
```

The Latent layer identifies, maintains, and organizes Reflective Opportunities.

---

# Relationship To Openings

A Reflective Opportunity is not an Opening.

An Opening is a user-facing invitation.

A Reflective Opportunity is an internal reflective possibility.

Multiple Openings may emerge from the same Opportunity.

Different Opportunities may lead to similar Openings.

---

# Relationship To Threads

A Reflective Opportunity is not a Thread.

A Thread is a reflective process.

Reflective Opportunities may initiate Threads.

Threads may refine Opportunities.

Threads may reveal new Opportunities.

Reflective Opportunities and Threads influence one another over time.

---

# Continuity Across Dreams

A Reflective Opportunity may persist across multiple dreams.

An Opportunity may continue as an evolving reflective thread-like structure.

However:

continuity must never override the current dream.

When an existing Opportunity reappears, the current dream should be treated as a new node in the evolving thread.

The system may identify continuation, resonance, or development.

The system must not assume unresolved processing, require continuation, or collapse a new dream into an existing Opportunity.

---

# Lifecycle

Reflective Opportunities are not static.

An Opportunity may:

```text
emerge
strengthen
weaken
split
merge
evolve
be abandoned
```

New dreams, continuity information, and reflections may alter an Opportunity over time.

---

# Multiplicity Principle

A dream may contain multiple Reflective Opportunities.

The Latent layer should preserve meaningful ambiguity whenever possible.

The goal is not to maximize Opportunity count.

The goal is to identify the most credible and potentially fruitful Opportunities supported by available evidence.

The Latent layer should not manufacture Opportunities merely to satisfy a target quantity.

---

# Final Authority Rule

A Reflective Opportunity is neither truth nor interpretation.

It is a structured possibility for reflection.

Its purpose is not to decide meaning.

Its purpose is to help create the conditions in which meaning may emerge.
