# Glossary V2 Continuity Admission Contract v1

## Status

Draft v1

Planning / Canon Layer

Not an implementation document.

Defines what may become a Glossary Continuity Entity and what may not.

---

# Purpose

The Glossary is not an Observation archive.

The Glossary is not a scene-summary repository.

The Glossary is not a storage layer for everything that appears in dreams.

The purpose of the Glossary is:

```text
To build and preserve the dreamer's personal continuity universe.
```

The Glossary should contain entities that may reappear, accumulate meaning, develop history, and become continuity anchors across multiple dreams.

---

# Core Principle

Observation answers:

```text
What happened?
```

Glossary answers:

```text
What may return?
```

Observation describes.

Glossary preserves continuity.

---

# Admission Philosophy

Not every Observation element deserves continuity status.

The appearance of an element inside a dream is not sufficient justification for becoming a Glossary candidate.

Candidate generation should be governed by continuity potential, not by mere presence.

---

# Admission Tiers

The Continuity Admission Layer classifies elements into three categories.

---

# Tier 1 — Immediate Admission

These elements possess continuity potential from their first appearance.

They may immediately become Glossary candidates.

Examples include:

* specific people
* named people
* recurring family members
* named places
* specific locations
* named animals
* specific objects with clear identity
* user-recognized personal references

Examples:

```text
Apa
Anya
Dóri
Réka
Pest
Mammut
Bodri
Gyermekkori ház
```

Rationale:

```text
These entities already possess individual identity.
```

They do not require recurrence evidence before candidate generation.

---

# Tier 2 — Recurrence-Gated Admission

These elements may become continuity entities, but not from a single appearance alone.

A recurrence signal is required before admission.

Examples:

```text
Lépcső
Folyosó
Ajtó
Erdő
Hegy
Szoba
Iskola
Templom
Ismeretlen táj
```

Rationale:

```text
Single appearances are usually scene construction.

Repeated appearances may indicate continuity.
```

The admission trigger is recurrence, not first occurrence.

---

# Tier 3 — Never Admission

These elements are not continuity entities.

They must never become Glossary candidates.

---

## System Perspective Elements

Examples:

```text
Dreamer
Narrator
Self
Observer
```

Rationale:

```text
These represent viewpoint, not continuity.
```

---

## Observation Emotional Labels

Examples:

```text
Fear
Threat
Tension
Anxiety
Relief
```

Rationale:

```text
Emotions belong to Observation and future reflective systems,
not Glossary continuity.
```

---

## Scene Descriptions

Examples:

```text
Closed building with strange stairs
Button pressed by father
People in large room
Slurry pouring through door
```

Rationale:

```text
These are descriptions of events or scenes,
not continuity entities.
```

---

## Narrative Fragments

Examples:

```text
Someone helping
Group standing nearby
Conversation taking place
```

Rationale:

```text
Narrative fragments describe dream activity.

They do not represent continuity anchors.
```

---

# Continuity Entity Definition

An element may become a Glossary entity when it can plausibly support:

```text
Identity
Persistence
Reappearance
Accumulated Meaning
```

All four are stronger indicators than simple presence inside a dream.

---

# Observation vs Glossary Boundary

Observation may record:

```text
Father presses a button.
```

Glossary may preserve:

```text
Father
```

Observation may record:

```text
A strange building with snowy stairs.
```

Glossary may preserve:

```text
Specific building
```

only if the building acquires continuity significance.

Observation stores descriptions.

Glossary stores continuity entities.

---

# Recurrence Principle

The Glossary follows the broader Lumira continuity philosophy:

```text
Continuity emerges through recurrence.
```

Many entities are not significant because they appeared.

They become significant because they return.

---

# Candidate Generation Rule

Candidate generation should not operate as:

```text
Observation Label
↓
Glossary Candidate
```

Candidate generation should operate as:

```text
Observation
↓
Continuity Admission Layer
↓
Glossary Candidate
```

The Admission Layer is the authority responsible for deciding whether an Observation output possesses continuity potential.

---

# Relationship to Appearance History

Admission and continuity are separate concerns.

An entity may:

```text
Appear once
```

without becoming an admitted continuity entity.

Conversely:

```text
An admitted entity
```

may later accumulate Appearance Records and continuity history.

Appearance History begins after admission.

Admission does not imply significance.

It only grants continuity eligibility.

---

# Language Independence

Admission decisions must not depend on display language.

Whether labels appear as:

```text
Father
```

or:

```text
Apa
```

the admission decision should remain identical.

Language presentation is a separate concern from continuity admission.

---

# Canonical Summary

The Glossary exists to preserve continuity, not description.

Immediate-admission entities possess identity from first appearance.

Recurrence-gated entities require repeated appearance before admission.

Narrative descriptions, emotional annotations, scene summaries, and system-perspective labels are never continuity entities.

The Continuity Admission Layer is the boundary that prevents Observation from becoming a Glossary mirror.
