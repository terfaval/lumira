# Lumira Reflective Opening Generation Policy v0

## 1. Reflective Opening Philosophy

A reflective opening is a context-grounded invitation to notice, revisit, or gently deepen reflective material.

Openings exist to support continuity without coercion. They are the user-facing transformation of internal cognition signals (observation + latent + salience + continuity memory), not direct exposure of internal inference.

Lumira prefers invitation over interrogation because reflective depth grows through agency, pacing, and ambiguity tolerance, not through question pressure.

Explicitly:

- opening != task
- opening != mandatory question
- opening != coaching prompt
- opening != symbolic interpretation
- opening != engagement mechanic

Core pacing principles:

- fewer, stronger, calmer openings
- silence is valid support
- no opening is better than a weak opening
- no urgency framing

## 2. Opening Categories

Not all openings should be questions. Category choice controls cognitive load and interaction density.

| Category | Description | Use conditions | Density implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reflective question | direct but gentle inquiry | clear local evidence and low interruption risk | medium |
| Juxtaposition | places two elements side-by-side | meaningful contrast/parallel exists | medium |
| Continuity noticing | points to recurrence/return | multi-signal continuity evidence | low-medium |
| Scene return | invites revisiting a specific scene/span | unresolved scene salience + source trace clarity | medium |
| Emotional noticing | names affect movement without verdict | affect transitions are evidence-backed | low-medium |
| Unresolved tension invitation | highlights unresolved line softly | tension appears in multiple signals | medium |
| Motif resonance | links current material to personal motif memory | glossary recurrence is strong and not suppressed | low |
| Gentle recall | reminds user of prior reflective anchor | thread/response history has relevant continuity | low |
| Perspective shift | invites alternate angle | single reading feels over-dominant | medium-high |
| Metacognitive noticing | invites noticing how reflection is happening | lucidity/metacognitive cues present | low-medium |
| Reflective silence/open space | explicitly leaves space without question | saturation or low-confidence context | minimal |
| Optional exploratory branch | suggests adjacent continuity line | neighborhood overlap is plausible but non-primary | medium |

## 3. Opening Generation Conditions

Openings are allowed when sufficient evidence supports user-beneficial surfacing.

Primary generation signals:

- strong highlight salience (especially user-confirmed/pinned)
- continuity recurrence across thread/highlight/motif
- unresolved affective or agency structures
- glossary recurrence with contextual relevance
- response gaps around active unresolved material
- repeated latent evidence across multiple cycles
- explicit revisit behavior
- user-created salience actions

Minimum evidence philosophy:

- a single weak latent signal is insufficient
- weak evidence stays internal (`generated`/`candidate`)
- surfaced openings require cross-signal support or strong direct user salience

Conceptual signal aggregation logic:

- source evidence quality gate
- salience gate (user-owned signals weighted highest)
- continuity gate (recurrence/trajectory support)
- pacing gate (density/cooldown/interruption checks)

Confidence and uncertainty handling:

- confidence remains internal scalar/ranking input
- surfaced language should carry uncertainty markers ("may", "seems", "might")
- low-confidence candidates should not be surfaced

## 4. Silence and Non-generation Rules

Silence is legitimate runtime behavior and often the best reflective support.

Do not generate/surface openings when:

- user is in uninterrupted writing flow
- evidence is weak or noisy
- opening density is already high
- recent repeated defer/dismiss exists
- user saturation signals are present
- continuity confidence is low/volatile
- recent resurfacing volume is high
- looped repetitive emotional pattern is already surfaced without new evidence

Contemplative restraint principles:

- avoid "always say something" behavior
- prefer ambient continuity over foreground prompting
- protect user reflective tempo over model activity

## 5. Surfacing and Timing Policy

Generation and surfacing are separate stages.

- generation stage: create internal opening candidates
- surfacing stage: decide if/when a candidate becomes visible

Foreground vs ambient logic:

- foreground: top bounded openings with strongest relevance and lowest interruption risk
- ambient/internal: valid but lower-priority candidates kept unsurfaced

Timing and spacing policy:

- apply cooldown after defer/dismiss
- avoid back-to-back opening bursts
- prefer natural transition moments (post-response, post-highlight, re-entry points)

Interruption safeguards:

- no surfacing during active long-form composition
- no notification-style urgency
- no aggressive resurfacing cadence

## 6. Invitation Grammar and Tone

Opening language must preserve ambiguity, agency, and non-authoritative stance.

Tone rules:

- calm
- non-diagnostic
- non-coercive
- evidence-linked
- optional

Preferred phrasing patterns:

- "Something here may connect to..."
- "You highlighted both moments where..."
- "This might relate to..."
- "Does this feel connected to...?"
- "If useful, you could stay with..."

Prohibited phrasing patterns:

- "This dream means..."
- "You are clearly..."
- "This proves..."
- "You need to..."
- "Your subconscious is telling you..."

Heuristics:

- anchor to concrete material (scene/highlight/motif/thread context)
- use uncertainty markers
- avoid absolute psychological claims
- preserve easy refusal/ignore path

## 7. Cognitive Load and Density Policy

Opening density must be explicitly bounded to preserve reflective spaciousness.

Policy:

- bounded foreground opening count per active context
- strict anti-flood safeguards
- prioritize strongest contextual invitations
- defer or suppress low-value openings

Calmness-preservation rules:

- less is often better
- do not surface all eligible continuity at once
- maintain quiet background continuity layer

Saturation handling:

- when candidate volume rises, raise threshold rather than surface more
- repeated non-engagement decreases future surfacing probability

## 8. Relationship to Threads and Continuity

Openings are continuity instruments attached to thread runtime, not standalone chat prompts.

Attachment semantics:

- openings should attach to a thread when continuity center exists
- unthreaded openings remain temporary and should either attach or expire

Continuity effects:

- good opening engagement can strengthen thread salience
- repeated defer/dismiss can reduce salience and surfacing priority
- openings may activate dormant threads via resurfacing rules

Neighborhood boundaries:

- openings may reference adjacent threads only when overlap evidence exists
- avoid broad multi-thread entanglement in one opening

## 9. Highlight and Glossary Interaction

User-owned salience is primary in opening generation.

Highlight interaction rules:

- user-confirmed highlights are high-priority opening anchors
- AI-suggested highlights require caution until confirmed
- repeated highlight clusters can justify continuity openings

Glossary interaction rules:

- glossary provides contextual recurrence cues
- glossary never overrides current dream evidence
- suppressed/do-not-surface motifs must gate opening surfacing

Anti-symbolic-overreach:

- motif recurrence is invitation context, not symbolic proof
- no universal meaning claims

## 10. Attention Lens Interaction

Attention lenses bias opening selection as soft weighting.

Rules:

- lens influence is probabilistic preference, not hard filter
- no mode-locking or deterministic exclusion
- lens effects should decay/rebalance over time

Anti-filter-bubble safeguards:

- periodically allow non-lens-matching high-salience material
- user can dismiss/disable lens effects
- preserve multi-reading continuity options

## 11. Non-authoritative Safeguards

Lumira supports reflection, not psychological verdict generation.

Mandatory safeguards:

- anti-diagnostic language policy
- anti-certainty phrasing policy
- ambiguity preservation defaults
- user authority over meaning
- revisability over closure

Internal vs surfaced distinction:

- latent confidence and alternatives stay internal
- surfaced openings communicate possibilities, not conclusions

Confidence handling policy:

- confidence affects internal ranking only
- uncertainty should be surfaced linguistically, not numerically

Anti-closure rule:

- openings should widen reflective possibility space, not collapse it

## 12. Failure Modes / Anti-patterns

Prohibited drift patterns:

- chatbot conversational addiction loops
- interrogation-feeling behavior
- productivity/task prompting
- pseudo-therapy authority stance
- excessive continuity resurfacing
- symbolic certainty claims
- forced emotional excavation
- over-questioning
- notification-like pressure
- engagement-optimization behavior
- compulsive always-on generation

Warning indicators:

- rising opening count with falling engagement
- repeated defer/dismiss on similar prompts
- repeated resurfacing without new evidence
- certainty-heavy phrasing frequency increase

Implementation cautions:

- separate generation policy from UI render cadence
- enforce gating before surfacing, not after
- keep single-source policy for suppression/defer semantics

| proactive notification channels for openings | POST-ALPHA | out of scope |

## 14. Runtime / Backend Implications

Orchestration boundaries:

- stage A: candidate generation (internal)
- stage B: policy gating (evidence/salience/pacing/silence checks)
- stage C: surfacing decision (foreground vs ambient/internal)
- stage D: lifecycle updates from user actions (engage/defer/dismiss/expire)

Opening-generation jobs:

- should be idempotent and policy-driven
- should emit source traces for surfaced candidates
- should respect suppression/do-not-surface constraints

Payload contract implications:

- `reflective_openings.payload` holds transformed invitation context
- `reflective_openings.source_trace` stores evidence lineage
- raw latent payload remains internal-only

Runtime adapter implications:

- bridge from legacy question/work outputs to opening lifecycle while cutover is active
- map legacy rejection events into `deferred/dismissed` opening semantics

API implications:

- explicit lifecycle actions: surface, engage, defer, dismiss, revisit, expire/archive
- explicit re-entry contract should return bounded openings only

Safety/moderation boundary:

- policy layer should block unsafe certainty/diagnostic framing at surfacing time
- moderation should not force engagement pressure patterns

Rebuild sequencing implications:

- keep bridge reads during transition
- avoid uncontrolled dual-write
- retire legacy opening/question paths only after parity validation
